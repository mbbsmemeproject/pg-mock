Privacy policy — Telangana NEET PG Mock Counselling (unofficial)

This is a free, unofficial practice tool. It is not connected to KNRUHS or any government body.

What we collect

  • Your Google account email address, used only to sign you in.
  • The NEET PG roll number you register, and the category, gender, minority and in-service details you confirm.
  • The college and course options you enter, and your mock allotment result.

How it is used

Only to run the mock allotment and show you your result. The organiser can see registrations, options and results in order to run the mock. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising.

Where it is stored

In a Supabase database. Each student can see only their own data.

Deleting your data

Ask the organiser and your registration, options and results will be deleted. All data is deleted after the mock ends.
